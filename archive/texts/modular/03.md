**Re-designing sound objects and spaces.**

Designing *sonic objects* and *sound spaces*. 
Towards a naturalization of abstract sonic experiences. We are creating specific site conditions and producing specific *sonic objects* able to give life and immediate expressive meaning and phisicality to our electroacoutic compositions.
