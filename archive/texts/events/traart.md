For whom who may be interested, 
students and professors from european and swiss universities and institutions 
 
The guideline of the workshop are the experiences gathered by the team Giardino Sonoro La Limonaia dell'Imperialino in its 2 years of activity. 
 
The contents of the workshop are 
the relationship between sound/lignt design and botanics, architectural planning and sound object design conditioned and inspired by a naturalistic site - urban or extra/urban
-; 
the methodological and linguistic construction of a professional multi-disciplinary team. 
 
The competences required by the workshop are mainly provided by architecture, media, music and digital arts schools. 
We are also very pleased if students interested on the contents of the workshop would seek to join us, though they hadn't a specific architectural, design, musical or electronic professional/training experience. The workshop has the goal to inter-cross attitudes and provoke different approaches to the presence and distribution of sound in an immersive site, and specifically to the various technologically immersive ways to experience the complexity of a contemporary garden. 
Planning/organizing life in a space is something that should involve the attention and passion of a radical inter-culture for immersive symbolic experiences.What we need from a student 
 
We need a formal application form, submitted by his school, 
confirming: 
- the student's position and his c.v.. 
- the official position of the student in the school 
- a request to attend the workshop CANTIERE GIARDINO SONORO - SONIC GARDEN WORKSHOP, 3-10 April 2005, Florence: 
 
Expenses:Sonic Garden workshop - organized by our team and the Tuscan Office for Cultural Affairs - covers the hotel and lunch expenses; 
Up to the invited institutions are the travel expenses and the dinners of their students. 
Also listeners positions are available.
