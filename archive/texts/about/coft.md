Filippo Tincolini was born in 1976, he lives and he works in carrara 
 
Since 1997 Tincolini collaborates with different artisans shops as the atelier of Orlandini in Lucca, the foundry Del Chiaro in Pietrasanta, the sculptor's studio Statuaria Marmi and S.G.F. in Carrara. 
He graduated in 2001 at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Carrara with teacher P.G.Balocchi. 
In 2001 Tincolini and sculptor Simone Zanaglia organize events of revaluation of abandoned marble mines near Macael (Spain). 
In 2002 he is invited at the first Symposium International de Scultura in Palma de Maiorca. 
In may 2003 he takes part as sculptor to the realization of a monumental park in Menorca. 
In the same year he starts with FIGAIA CAVE s.p.a an artistic collaboration in order to revaluate anthropical meaning of the marble excavation area.
