Paolo
Frasconi is Associate Professor of Computer Science
with the Università di Firenze, Italy. His research
interests include machine learning and neural networks
with applications to bioinformatics, natural language
and pattern recognition. He worked as a professional
musician in the early 80's and joined Timet since its
early conception. His contributions include some productions
and with a few compositions.
