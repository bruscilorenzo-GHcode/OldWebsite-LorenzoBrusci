Timet
perspectives on Classicism.

1- The idea of classicism refers to a cognitive
public frame. We intend to criticize a static idea of
music heritage.

We are suggesting an ETHIC OF USING and ABUSING of cultural heritage.

We also defend a sophisticated preservation of the access to and availability of all the original sources at any processing level.

2- Memory as a collective memory.

A dialectical, dynamic and actually electronic confrontation between music
histories
exemplifies the circle of cultural synthesis .

Timet is against a strict and not-symbolic author-right legislation.
