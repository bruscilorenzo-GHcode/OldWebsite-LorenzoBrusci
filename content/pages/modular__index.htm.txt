Timet
Pre-conditions.

Info-structure

Timet is the story of the creation of
a symbolic system. After 10 years

of research and experimentations, we can begin to outline
the elements of

an aesthetic and conceptual extension, which follow and
introduce all the

works of the group.

Our History is mainly a music history. In the last years
we have been applying our methods to any
symbolic language, chasing a multi-linguistic artwork
we are still building up.

Methodology.

- Art of Data Transfiguration .

.Digital editing as Art of Data Transfiguration; our
operating tools are analogue and digital data
processing and design.

.Generating complex materials re-editable for multiple
relationships. Inside and outside Timet.

Complex means the hybrid dimension of a music aggregate,
constituted by electro-edited data and natural sources
(like
acoustic musician’s interpretations): complex materials
and the single note belong to the same potential and
common world of
sonic experience.

.Editing, processing and inter-mixing imply that any
compositional identity is a dynamic, multiply
referenced and nonlinear status.

.we are building a symbolic open database, where all
the data are available for multiple and nonlinear compositions:

To free compositions and their sources is a methodological
and ethic decision, in order to increase an active, critical,
interrelated and extended idea of symbolic systems.

Comprehension through editing : acting in a dynamic and
editable symbolic world.

To Use : free re-contextualization of any music reference.

To refer : pointing out relevancies and analytic links
to pure and elementary sources.

People networking

From sound design and music composition to sound gardening
and digital architecture. Timet is open
to different collaborations and linguistic perspectives.
