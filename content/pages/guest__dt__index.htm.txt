Davide Tidoni (Brescia, 1981).

While studying drums, he realized that the instrument
wasn't made just to keep the beat; the beat should be
kept in one's head anyway.

Davide loves it when amplifiers fizzle-out and break
in an orgy of noise, or when old crazies speak to him.

He unfortunatly hates most of the other things one sees
in an average day.

Playing mostly by himself, he has produced works for
prepared drums, objects and electroacoustic music.

The two tracks you can listen to are taken from “Hardgore”,
a work for prepared drumset and electronics (2003)
