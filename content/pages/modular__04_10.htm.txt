SAMPLING Art

The 5 re-cyclying totems

.a metamusic quintet.

sampling art by Lorenzo Brusci

5 totems by artists Giampaolo Di Cocco, Filippo Tincolini,
Simone Zanaglia, Giacomo Bonciolini

Sound Installation.

Sampled composers, among others: F. Zappa, G. Clinton,
G. Ligeti, S. Barrett, Bach, L. Berio, N. Cave, A. Artaud,
J. Coltrane, Bomporti, G. Petrassi, K. Penderecki, Beatles,
O. Coleman, B. Eno, Pergolesi, Monteverdi.

“…
is it possible to give a synthetical and analogue short
impression of an entire compositional life?

Inside the sonic totems built by our visual collaborators,
we locate our music meta-bodies, results of a radical
sampling art.

We tried out real concentrations of music experiences Totems are syncronic,
they set up a real meta-music quintet…”.

No technical needs.
