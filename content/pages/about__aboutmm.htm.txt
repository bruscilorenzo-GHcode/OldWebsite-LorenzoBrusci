Matteo
Milani – 1974 - is a sound designer living in
Milan. Actually he works in a post production facility,
editing audio for cinema, tv and radio commercials.

He started his career at the end of the 80’s
in local radio stations, playing, editing and mixing
music in “analogue” mode. He focused on
dance projects, producing and applying electronic music
on his dj activity, sequencing audio samples in his
sessions. In the mid 90’s he pioneered the rising
of digital audio workstations as Digidesign Pro Tools
creative user. In the last years he studied and developed
a deep interest in relationships between sound and
picture. He joined Timet, reflecting most of his sensibility
creating sound elements for new forms of art expression.

Collaborations:

- Game Programming Italia ( www.gameprog.it ),
as a writer for interactive audio;

- Symbolic Sound Corporation, home of the Kyma sound
design language ( www.symbolicsound.com ),
as a third party samples supplier.

“…from the beginning of my creative process,
like recording a real sound source, the same source
suggests to me what is could became: I have discovered
that, from my way of interpreting sound design, the
best way to treat sound is to start from a ‘living” source,
that remind us something familiar.

Even though I am not a musician, I have always loved
experimenting to build sound textures usign both noise
and music elements, trying to fill the gap between
these two worlds…”.

You may find samples of his work visiting graphicalsound
