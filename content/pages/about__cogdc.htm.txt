Born
September, 6, 1947 in Florence, Italy Lives and works
as artist and architect in Florence, Cologne and Berlin.

1968-75

studies architecture at the University of Florence

1975

winner of the Foundation Pontello prize, Florence

1976

distinction at the national competition for the Centro
Direzionale of Castello.

1977

founds the magazine 'Abaco' for contemporary art and
architecture.

1980

collaborates on the culture pages of the 'Corriere
Medico', Milan: writing the column 'Letter from Berlin'
interviews Paolo Portoghesi, Renzo Piano, Aldo Rossi
and other architecture

1981

winner of the Fondation Lubiam prize Mantua

1983-87

studies at the design Polytechnic, Cologne

Texts and features about architecture

1987-89

collaborates with architectural magazines for architecture
as 'Alfabeta', 'Urbanistica Informazioni'' and so on

1989

'City Parks in Potsdam, Berlin, Amsterdam", is
published by the county administration of Tuscany

1993-2002

publication of various texts in the magazine for the
Association of architects of Florence, 'Professione
Architetto' and 'Bollettino degli Architetti'

Planing and project direction

(selection)

1985-90

architecture for exhibitions, among others for the
exhibition "L'Etrusco contemporaneo" at the
Palazzo degli Innocenti, Florence

1992-95

installation of several works on the grounds of the
Villa Imbarcati-Gori, Pistoia

1995-'00

installation of several works in the residential complex
at Neusser Platz 10, Cologne Projects for the installation
of many works on the ground of the "Business Park" in
Duisburg, Germany, under commission of the Deutsche
Bank.

2001

Competition for the new Art Museum of Bolzano, Italy.

2002

Competition for the new egiptyan museum of Cairo

2003

Competition for the new palace of the County Administration
of Pisa, Italy

Artistic works and exhibitions

(selection)

1972-75

produces experimental 8mm films

1973-75

first group and solo exhibitions in Florence and the
surrounding area(among others, at the Galleries Il
Fiore, L'indiano etc.) begins the series of 'Light-pictures'

1976-87

produces pictures using various materials, playing
with the transition from the two-dimensional to the
three-dimensional; large-scale pictures with colors
appliede spontaneously and directly; series: "Lettera
di Leptis", "La Nuovissima Biblioteca d'Alessandria", "Storie
della città-albero" first works with the
themes "Grandi Naufragi/Large Shipwrecks" and "Gates
of Heaven"

since 1987

various exhibitions in Europe, e.g. at the Galleries
Tardy (Enschede/Netherlands), Gisele Linder (Basel,
Switzerland), Apicella (Bonn, Germany), at the Galleria
d'Arte di Bologna, Italy, at the Scuola Grande di S.Teodoro
(Venice, Italy).

since 1988

Continues with various installations the series of "Grandi
Naufragi", e.g. at the Breda Works in Pistoia,
Italy (1988), at the stadium's station Olympia, Berlin,
Germany 1990- exhibition presented by Dr. Lucie Schauer,
director of the Neuer Berliner Kunstverein- at the
Villa Romana, Florence, Italy, at the BBK, Cologne,
Germany (1992) at the French Institute, Florence (1994),
at Skagen, Denmark (1995), at the Gallery Cargo, Marseille,
France (1995), Gallery Vito Vitti, Bari, Italy (1997),
at the Rathenau Center and Culture and Technology in
Berlin-Kopenick (1998).

since 1996

Various projects for an artistic development of public
buildings and places, realizations e.g. at Follonica,
Tuscany ("Grandi Naufragi XIII" 1996-'97,
development of a public square, with fountain and sculpture)
works as architect, specialized in urbanism and enviromental
planning.

1999

Solo exibition and installation "Car-Naval/Atlantis
II" at the Gallery Exit Art, Cologne, Germany.

2000

Permanent installation "Car-Naval/He 111" in
the Business Park of Duisburg, Germany.

2002

Installation "Haus von Paulus", Kunstverein "Skulptur
draussen", Cologne
