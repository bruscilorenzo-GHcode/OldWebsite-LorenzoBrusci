(Firenze
Italy 1971). His background is mainly technical. In
the nineties he worked as a designer of fountain pens
but has never neglected his love and intrest for the
digital arts and the simulation of architectural spaces
in the software ambient.

Towards the end of the nineties he worked and created
the webpage of Marco
Parente wich, during the year
2002, was awarded as one of the Best 100 webpages by
the Italian
Internet Web Award .

In collaboration with Graziano Staino he started the
Blindvision project which occupies with video production
and, using the net, the creation of an alternative
network to the usual art circuit in Florence.

In the year 2003 he was again awarded by the Italian
Internet Web Award for the web page of Marco Parente
as one of the Excellent sites of Italy.

Meanwhile,
he has continued his interest and activity in architectural
simulation working in collaboration with several architects.

This year, 2003, taking part of the Opensongs project
he has now become member of the concept group Timet
for which he now collaborates and has realised the
homepage.
