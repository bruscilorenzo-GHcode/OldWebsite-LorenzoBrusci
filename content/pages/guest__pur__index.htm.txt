jan 2004 | purusha

Purusha is born in 1999 from Emanuele Rodolà and
is currently supported

and complemented by the work of Luca Balsanelli and Valentina
Gualtieri

on the instrumental and vocal side respectively. Further
collaborations with

other artists are not sporadic and also extend the spectrum
of sonorities and
composing techniques he is interested in.

Up until now he has released different works for labels
such as

Eerik Inpuj Sound, Hydrogen Dukebox, Saag Records and
Ogredung, which

he is the founder and runner of.

Purusha analyzes sound and develops romantic and abstract
unions.
