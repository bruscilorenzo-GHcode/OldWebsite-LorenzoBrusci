Performance and Sound installation. Mirio Cosottini-Timet

Mirio Cosottini – Timet

Composition, piano, voice

Lorenzo Brusci – Timet

Electronic transfiguration, sound design, installation

Lorenzo Brusci – Timet

Mirio Cosottini - Timet

Acoustic concept

Loudspeakers designed by sculptur Filippo
Tincolini and sound architect Andres Bosshard

Mirio Cosottini and Lorenzo
Brusci work from different
point of views on the same thematic content: The
Zibaldone by G. Leopardi .

On Cosottini's side we have the acoustic experience
of voice and piano in a clear and transparent real
time composition; on the other side, Brusci's electronic
world extends the spatial dimension of sounds and their
distribution, specially thanks to the many possibilities
offered by multichannel sound speaker systems. A strong
and expressive experience, due to the extreme use of
the voice in a sonic ambience that reacts to an inner
and violent textual stimulus.

The electro-acoustic Timet production team deals with
a performative task, paying attention to the quality
and design of sound materials, connected to a rigorous
compositive and interpretative process.
