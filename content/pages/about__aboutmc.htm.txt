Mirio
Cosottini graduated from Florence¹s Academy of
Music in 1992 and subsequently studied under P. Thibaud. In
1999, he received his degree in Philosophy at the University
of Florence. He is one of the founding members of Timet,
a 10-year collaboration that led to his contribution
as a performer and composer on several of the group¹s
highly original works. Since that time, he has
delved further into composition, musical analysis and
improvisation, also evidenced in his musical contributions
to the fields of dance (Raffaella Giordano ­ Sosta
Palmizi, Simone Sandroni Dejadonne), theatre (Massimo
Luconi), poetry (Giancarlo Majorino), and cinema (soundtrack
music for "Anche le donne hanno perso la guerra" directed
by Carlo Bolli for Rai Sat network).

He has worked with many contemporary and classical artists,
among them Giorgio Gaber, Irene Grandi, Marco
Parente ,
Andrea Chimenti , Ares Tavolazzi, Stefano
Bollani , Mirko
Guerrini, Monica Demuru, Andrea De Luca, Zeena
Parkins ,
Frank Shulte, Werner Puntigam, Italian Young Orchestra,
Bruno De Franceschi, Fulvio Caldini, Maurizio Ben Omar,
Oliviero Lacagnina, Vinko Globocar , Eliott
Sharp .
